Balldozer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Balldozer (“the app”, “the game”) is developed and operated by Hodgepodge Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the game collects and how it is used. We collect only the minimum information needed to run the game. Questions: support@hodgepodge.studio.

1. Information we collect

Automatically, when you play

  • Anonymous account ID — Firebase Authentication creates a random user ID so your progress can be saved on our servers. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Device-stable identifier — your Apple Vendor ID (IDFV) or Android ID, used only so you can recover your account if you reinstall the game on the same device.
  • Game data — coins, gems, owned ball skins, power-ups, daily-reward streak, mission progress, best score, and your settings (sound, music, haptics).
  • IP address — automatically logged by Firebase, and by AdMob where ads are shown, for security and abuse prevention. It is never displayed to other players.

That you provide

  • Nickname — shown on the public global leaderboard and visible to other players. Nicknames are limited to 1–16 characters and are checked against a blocked-words list on our server before being saved. You can change yours at any time in Settings.

From Apple, Google, or Toss when you make a purchase

  • Transaction receipts — the store sends us a signed receipt so we can verify the purchase and grant the item. We do not receive your card number, billing address, or your Apple / Google / Toss account details.

Tracking

We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Balldozer does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the iOS Advertising Identifier (IDFA); the app’s privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false. Where ads are shown they are non-personalized. Ad and platform providers may still collect device and usage signals under their own policies (see section 3).

2. How we use information

  • Run the game — save your progress, currencies, and unlocks across sessions and devices
  • Operate the global leaderboard, daily rewards, and missions
  • Verify in-app purchases and grant the corresponding items
  • Show optional rewarded video ads and grant their rewards
  • Prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse (including score validation and purchase-replay protection)

We do not use your data for advertising profiles or for automated decisions that produce legal effects.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions — policy
  • Google AdMob — rewarded video ads — policy
  • Apple Game Center (iOS) / Google Play Games (Android) — optional native leaderboard submission
  • Apple StoreKit, Google Play Billing, Apps in Toss — purchase processing and receipt verification, handled by the platform

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your nickname and best score appear on the public leaderboard by design. We may disclose data only where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as you keep using the game. If you delete your data (see section 6) it is removed from our systems within 30 days. Anti-fraud records such as processed transaction identifiers may be kept longer, without profile data attached, to prevent purchase replay.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Delete — Settings → “Delete My Data” removes your account and its server-side data. This cannot be undone.
  • Access / portability — email us for a copy of the data linked to your account.
  • Correct — change your nickname at any time in Settings.
  • Transfer — Settings → “Recovery Code” issues a code that moves your account to another device. Keep it private: anyone holding it can claim the account.
  • Object / restrict — email us to ask that we stop processing your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@hodgepodge.studio. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children’s privacy

Balldozer is a casual arcade game suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat or user-to-user messaging. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our servers run on Google Cloud in the asia-northeast3 (Seoul) region. Apple, Google, Toss, and ad providers may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

9. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Server-side rules restrict who can read or write each record, and currency balances, purchases, and leaderboard scores are validated on our servers rather than trusted from the device. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent change. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
